UndergroundScout v0.8 - Google Sheets Feedback Setup

Use this if testers are on different computers/networks and you want all feedback in one shared Sheet.

1) Create a Google Sheet.
2) Go to Extensions > Apps Script.
3) Paste this code:

function doPost(e) {
  var sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().getSheetByName('Feedback');
  if (!sheet) {
    sheet = SpreadsheetApp.getActiveSpreadsheet().insertSheet('Feedback');
    sheet.appendRow([
      'timestamp','tester_name','dj_type','rating','comment','artist','track','genre','track_type','platform','url','artist_url','artwork_url','score','confidence','plays','likes','reposts','hidden_gem','label','why_picked'
    ]);
  }
  var data = JSON.parse(e.postData.contents);
  sheet.appendRow([
    data.timestamp || new Date().toISOString(),
    data.tester_name || '',
    data.dj_type || '',
    data.rating || '',
    data.comment || '',
    data.artist || '',
    data.track || '',
    data.genre || '',
    data.track_type || '',
    data.platform || '',
    data.url || '',
    data.artist_url || '',
    data.artwork_url || '',
    data.score || '',
    data.confidence || '',
    data.plays || '',
    data.likes || '',
    data.reposts || '',
    data.hidden_gem || '',
    data.label || '',
    data.why_picked || ''
  ]);
  return ContentService.createTextOutput(JSON.stringify({status:'ok'})).setMimeType(ContentService.MimeType.JSON);
}

4) Click Deploy > New deployment.
5) Type: Web app.
6) Execute as: Me.
7) Who has access: Anyone.
8) Copy the Web App URL.
9) Paste it into UndergroundScout > Feedback Settings > Google Sheet Webhook URL.
10) Turn on "Send feedback online" and save.

If the URL is blank or fails, UndergroundScout still saves feedback locally inside data/underground_scout.db and can export a feedback CSV.
