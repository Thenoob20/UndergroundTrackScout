WELCOME

• Hidden Gems
• Rising Artists

• New Music Before It Hits the Mainstream

===================

Operating System:
• Windows 10
• Windows 11

Internet:
• Required

Mac Users:

====================================
INSTALLATION GUIDE
==================

STEP 1

Download:

UndergroundScout_v0_8_4.zip

from the GitHub Releases page.

---

STEP 2

Locate the downloaded file.

Most users will find it in:

Downloads

Example:

C:\Users\YourName\Downloads

---

STEP 3

Right-click:

UndergroundScout_v0_8_4.zip

Select:

Extract All...

---

STEP 4

Choose where you want to extract the files.

Recommended:

Desktop

or

Documents

Click:

Extract

---

STEP 5

Open the newly extracted folder.

You should see:

UndergroundTrackScout.exe

README_FIRST.txt

and additional application files.

---

STEP 6

Double-click:

UndergroundTrackScout.exe

---

STEP 7

Windows Defender may display a warning.

If prompted:

Click:

More Info

Then click:

Run Anyway

This application is currently unsigned and may trigger a Windows security warning.

====================================
FIRST SCAN
==========

1. Enter a genre.

Examples:

Tech House
Latin House
Afro House
Deep House
Minimal
House

---

2. Select Discovery Mode.

Recommended:

Deep Dig

---

3. Select Strictness.

Recommended:

Balanced

---

4. Click:

SCAN

---

5. Wait for results.

The scan may take a few moments depending on your internet connection.

====================================
UNDERSTANDING SCORES
====================

💎 Hidden Gem
Score 90 - 100

Rare underground discovery with strong potential.

---

🔥 Strong Pick
Score 75 - 89

High-quality discovery worth checking.

---

👍 Worth Checking
Score 50 - 74

Potentially useful track.

---

⚠ Mainstream
Score 25 - 49

Already gaining traction or less unique.

---

❌ Low Priority
Score 0 - 24

Lower recommendation.

====================================
TIPS
====

• Double-click a track to open it.
• Right-click a track for additional actions.
• Favorite tracks you would actually play.
• Like and Dislike tracks to train recommendations.
• Check Rising Artists regularly.
• Check Label Watch for new underground labels.
